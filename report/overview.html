Five of the six models with a trained thinking mode improved. Neither model that only had reasoning asked for in the prompt did. That holds across two GPUs, two serving stacks and four vendors — a stronger claim than any single number here, since the stacks share nothing but the task set and the grader.

The exception is instructive. GLM 4.7 Flash is the only model whose sampling was taken from the vendor's shipped default rather than measured on this suite — and it is the only native arm to lose ground. It also churned 112 tasks getting there (53 gained, 59 lost) against 38 for Gemma 4 26B at matched sampling. Roughly three times the movement for a worse total is the signature of the temperature change, not of reasoning: its off arm ran greedy and its on arm at t1.0. Until its temperature is measured the way every other model's was, −6 is not evidence that thinking fails on GLM — and at a noise floor of ~6 tasks it is barely distinguishable from zero anyway.

Prompted CoT is also the least stable measurement in the set. Rewording the instruction once moved Qwen2.5-Coder by 30 tasks, so −3 and −9 should be read as "no help, possibly harm" rather than as precise quantities. The rejected-wording runs are committed alongside so that claim is checkable.

What can and cannot be compared

  • Scores are comparable across both stacks. Identical task set and identical graders, and the graders were re-validated after being rebuilt for arm64 — reference oracle 600/600, null oracle 0/600, plus 60/60 and 0/60 on the long-context set, all matching the CUDA results exactly.
  • Speeds are not comparable. Different GPUs, different runtimes, different quantisations, and MLX runs single-stream because concurrency buys nothing there — 4 concurrent requests aggregate 84 tok/s against 79 single-stream, where CUDA scaled 22 → 83 tok/s.
  • Deltas are more comparable than absolute scores. Each is a within-model, within-stack difference, so it cancels most of what differs between the two setups.
  • The noise floor is measured, not assumed. Re-running an identical arm at a different context window flips ~6 tasks for a net change of 0. On CUDA, batching alone flipped 25 tasks for a net −3. So +12 and −3 sit near the floor; +26, +29 and +46 clear it comfortably.

Open items. GLM 4.7 Flash's reasoning arm is still running. The Qwen3.6 off arms ran at unrecorded context windows and are queued to be re-run at matched ones — Gemma's equivalent re-run flipped 6 tasks and landed on the same total. The 60-probe long-context suite is deferred to a later stage.

Why these numbers are trustworthy

  • A null oracle sits opposite the reference oracle. The reference run of every correct answer must score 600/600; a run that answers nothing must score 0/600. The second caught a task whose setup satisfied its own check — passable by saying nothing — which no amount of reference-checking would have found.
  • Sampling is measured per model. Greedy thinking works on some models and spirals on others, and the pattern does not follow the family: Qwen3.6 27B wants greedy while its own MoE sibling needs t1.0/k64, where hotter is simultaneously more reliable and cheaper, because a spiral burns the whole budget and returns nothing.
  • Every serving quirk that could fake a result was tested for. chat_template_kwargs is silently dropped on MLX — thinking absent, false and true return byte-identical output — so the arms are switched with reasoning_effort instead. Two Qwen3.6 models would otherwise have been classed as non-thinking and routed to the CoT arm, whose baseline would also have been thinking.
  • Decisions are recorded with their evidence. Each model's sampling choice lives in a chosen_*.json with the measurements behind it, including the cases where the automatic pick was overridden and why.
